# Infrastructure Committee Operational Charter

## Purpose

The purpose of the Infrastructure Committee is to improve the reliability, security, transparency, and sustainability of CHAOSS's existing technology infrastructure. The committee exists to ensure that CHAOSS infrastructure is governed through documented processes rather than individual ownership, reducing operational risk and ensuring continuity of the project over time.

The committee operates under the direction of the Board and provides recommendations, documentation, and implementation support within its approved scope.

## Definitions

### CHAOSS Infrastructure

For the purposes of this charter, CHAOSS infrastructure refers to the technical platforms, services, systems, accounts, and administrative resources that support the operation, governance, communication, and delivery of the CHAOSS project.

This includes, but is not limited to:

* Collaboration and communication platforms (e.g., Slack, Zoom, Gmail, Teamup).  
* Source code and development platforms (e.g., GitHub organizations and repositories).  
* Hosting, cloud, and infrastructure services (e.g., DigitalOcean, GreenGeeks, domains, DNS, and web hosting).  
* Website and content management platforms (e.g., WordPress, Moodle, YouTube).  
* Security and credential management systems (e.g., LastPass or other approved credential management solutions).  
* Social media accounts (e.g., X, LinkedIn, Mastodon, Bluesky, and Threads).  
* Financial and administrative platforms used to support the project (e.g., Open Collective).  
* Other technical services, shared accounts, systems, or infrastructure owned or managed on behalf of the CHAOSS project.

## Committee Objectives

The Infrastructure Committee will:

* Ensure CHAOSS infrastructure is owned, documented, and managed in the best interests of the project rather than individual contributors.  
* Establish and maintain appropriate access controls based on the principle of least privilege.  
* Develop policies, processes, and controls for managing project infrastructure consistently and securely.  
* Reduce operational and security risks associated with infrastructure ownership, permissions, credentials, and service management.  
* Maintain documentation of infrastructure assets, ownership, access, and operational procedures.  
* Review and approve infrastructure changes within its delegated authority.  
    
* Make infrastructure decisions collaboratively, drawing on the diverse expertise of committee members.  
* Provide recommendations to the Board on infrastructure improvements that fall outside the committee's delegated authority.

## In Scope

The committee is responsible for the governance and oversight of the CHAOSS infrastructure, including policies relating to ownership, access, security, documentation, and infrastructure management. Responsibility for the day-to-day administration and operational management of individual platforms remains with the relevant maintainers, working groups, or designated service owners, unless otherwise directed by the Board.

Within this scope, the committee's responsibilities include:

* Auditing infrastructure owned or used by CHAOSS.  
* Reviewing and managing access permissions.  
* Establishing infrastructure policies and operational procedures.  
* Documenting infrastructure ownership, credentials management, and access records.  
* Reviewing and approving infrastructure changes within the committee's authority.  
* Recommending improvements to strengthen reliability, security, and sustainability.  
* Maintaining an inventory of infrastructure services and responsible maintainers.  
* Reviewing infrastructure risks and recommending mitigation measures.  
* Developing policies and coordinating the response to incidents involving CHAOSS infrastructure, including administrative accounts, shared services, credentials, and access management.

## Out of Scope

Unless specifically authorised by the Board, the committee will not:

* Initiate major infrastructure redesigns or migrations.  
* Adopt new infrastructure platforms or tools solely because they are technically preferable.  
* Create new infrastructure initiatives unrelated to governance, security, or sustainability.  
* Manage security incidents or vulnerabilities relating to project software, codebases, or applications, except where they directly involve CHAOSS infrastructure or are specifically assigned by the Board.  
* Maintain recurring public infrastructure meetings that carry out core committee responsibilities.   
* Approve project expenditure or enter into contracts.  
* Make decisions outside infrastructure governance.  
* Override decisions made by the Board.

Any recommendations that fall outside the approved scope should be documented and submitted to the Board for consideration.

# Expected Deliverables

The committee is expected to produce and maintain:

* A current inventory of CHAOSS infrastructure.  
* Documentation of infrastructure ownership and responsible maintainers.  
* A documented access register showing who has administrative access to each system.  
* Infrastructure governance policies.  
* Credential and secrets management procedures.  
* Infrastructure change approval procedures.  
* Incident response and recovery procedures.  
* Recommendations for addressing identified risks.  
* Periodic reports to the Board on committee activities, risks, and progress.

# Governance

* The committee operates under the authority of the Board.  
* Infrastructure policies require approval by at least two-thirds of committee members before being submitted to the Board.  
* Infrastructure changes within the committee's delegated authority require approval by a majority of committee members.  
* Any proposal requiring funding must be referred to the Finance Committee and the Board for approval before implementation.  
* All committee decisions and votes should be documented.

# Initial Priorities

The committee's immediate priorities are to complete the foundational governance work that motivated its creation.

This includes:

1. Complete the audit of CHAOSS infrastructure and services.   
2. Review the responses from the infrastructure survey and use the findings to guide the committee's work.   
3. Document all infrastructure, including who owns it and who has access to it.   
4. Document all expenses and who is paying for it.  
5. Review existing access permissions and remove unnecessary or outdated access where appropriate.   
6. Develop the core policies needed to govern and manage CHAOSS infrastructure.   
7. Define and document standard processes for managing infrastructure consistently.  
8. Identify infrastructure risks and provide recommendations to the Board on how to reduce or address them.

Only after these foundational activities are completed should the committee consider broader infrastructure improvements or new initiatives unless directed otherwise by the Board.

# Future Amendments

This charter may be amended by the Board as the needs of the CHAOSS project evolve.

The committee may recommend amendments where clarification of responsibilities or governance is required.
